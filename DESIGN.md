## Brand & Style

The brand evokes timeless romance, couture craftsmanship, and high-fashion editorial sensibility. Designed for discerning couples, event planners, and luxury patrons, the interface feels like an archival art book or a high-end bridal publication rather than a commercial utility.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous, intentional negative space that allows photography and cinema stills to breathe.
- High-contrast, literary serif display typography paired with whisper-quiet, architectural sans-serif micro-labels.
- Quiet, deliberate interactions that move with cinematic ease rather than aggressive velocity.
- Restraint over embellishment: depth is achieved through translucent parchment layers, delicate hairline divisions, and subtle ambient glows rather than heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The color architecture is rooted in light, organic warmth, counterbalanced by deep charcoal slate to create gravitas and museum-grade contrast.

- **Primary (`#1C1C1E` - Charcoal Noir):** Used for authoritative editorial typography, high-impact titles, primary buttons, and framing borders. Never pure black, ensuring soft tonal cohesion with imagery.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` - Warm Champagne Gold):** A desaturated, metallic-tinged accent used for delicate badges, bespoke callouts, active indicators, and gilded framing lines.
- **Tertiary (`#8A7E72` - Sepia Slate):** Serves as secondary copy, dates, subtle metadata, and muted borders.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5` - Warm Ivory / Alabaster):** The foundation canvas. It acts as fine-art watercolor paper, softening user fatigue and enhancing warm photographic skin tones.
- **Surface Elevation Tints:**
  - Canvas / Foundation: `#FBF9F5`
  - Elevated Card / Container: `#FFFFFF`
  - Layered Surface / Inset: `#F3EFEA`

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses the tension between classic romance and sleek modernism:

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** An Italian high-fashion transitional serif. Characterized by dramatic vertical stress and sharp hairline serifs. Reserved for hero statements, chapter headings, and collection titles. Display scales leverage negative tracking to enhance the refined editorial voice.
- **Body & Labels (`Manrope`):** A geometric, humanist sans-serif. In light weights (`300`), it acts as an effortless, crystalline partner to Bodoni Moda. 
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Always uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.14em` to `0.22em`), evoking haute couture tags, gallery plaques, and curated exhibition captions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column asymmetric fluid grid that prioritizes breathing room, visual pauses, and dynamic storytelling over dense information packing.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and `4rem` outer canvas margins. Full-bleed imagery alternates with narrow, centered textual columns (spanning 6 to 8 columns) to replicate the cadence of a print monograph.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Editorial typography shifts to mobile-specific scales to preserve rhythm and eliminate orphaned lines.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing between major editorial sections utilizes `space-xl` (and multiples thereof, up to `8rem` for scene transitions), enforcing calm contemplation. Component internals remain strictly bound to the 4px baseline rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy, computer-generated drop shadows in favor of **low-contrast outlines, tactile paper overlays, and subtle ambient glows**.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat Warm Ivory (`#FBF9F5`) devoid of borders.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Editorial Panels):** Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale alabaster (`#F3EFEA`) enclosed by a delicate 1px hairline border in Sepia Slate at 18% opacity (`rgba(138, 126, 114, 0.18)`). No drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Overlays, Floating Badges):** Pure white backing layered with an ultra-diffuse warm ambient shadow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(28, 28, 30, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Cinema Lightboxes):** Glassmorphic Charcoal backdrop: `rgba(28, 28, 30, 0.72)` with a 16px backdrop blur, placing total dramatic focus on the imagery.

## Shapes

The shape architecture commits to **0 (Sharp)**:
- True rectangular discipline reflecting museum frames, gallery mats, and trimmed archival print stock.
- Every container, input field, button, and image canvas features razor-sharp 90-degree corners (`0px` radius).
- Rounded pill forms are strictly prohibited, ensuring that the interface never drifts into casual, playful, or consumer-tech visual tropes.
- Small decorative exceptions are limited exclusively to functional circle accents (such as audio/film playheads and bullet markers), while all structural UI remains architectural and squared.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Charcoal Noir (`#1C1C1E`) fill with Warm Ivory text (`#FBF9F5`). Rectangular shape, uppercase `label-md` typography with `0.18em` letter spacing. Padding: `1rem 2.25rem`. Hover state introduces a smooth color shift to Warm Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`) or a subtle inset border.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA:** Transparent background, crisp 1px hairline border in Charcoal Noir (`#1C1C1E`) or Gold (`#C5A880`). Text matches border color. Hover shifts background to `#1C1C1E` with inverted typography.
- **Editorial Link:** Naked text with an animated 1px underline positioned 4px below baseline that expands from left to right on interaction.

### Badges & Editorial Tags
- Thin, delicate framing: 1px border in `#C5A880`, transparent background, with `label-sm` typography in Charcoal Noir or Warm Gold. 
- Padding: `0.35rem 0.85rem`. Used for package designations (e.g., "COLLECTION I", "CINEMATIC CURATION") and gallery categorization.

### Pricing Cards & Investment Collections
- Background in pure `#FFFFFF` resting against the `#FBF9F5` canvas, bordered by a single hairline line (`rgba(138, 126, 114, 0.2)`).
- Generous internal padding (`3rem 2.5rem`).
- Price numbers set in `Bodoni Moda` Display scales with small currency markers in `Manrope` uppercase.
- Feature lists separated by ultra-light dividers (`1px solid rgba(138, 126, 114, 0.12)`) featuring serene row spacing (`space-md`).

### Form Inputs & Text Areas
- Minimalist underline or boxed border styling: a solitary 1px bottom rule in Sepia Slate (`rgba(138, 126, 114, 0.4)`), expanding to a solid Charcoal Noir 1px focus state.
- Floating uppercase labels (`label-md`) resting above the field with generous vertical margin.
- Transparent background to absorb the ivory paper surface.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Crisp 14px sharp squares (`0px` radius) with 1px borders in `#1C1C1E`.
- Selected state features a solid center square inset by 2px, avoiding standard digital checkmarks in favor of archival draft marks.

### Gallery & Media Tiles
- Razor-sharp image containers with zero rounding.
- Hover interactions feature a cinematic, subtle zoom (`scale(1.02)`) accompanied by an ethereal transition from 90% to 100% opacity, revealing title metadata formatted in italicized `Bodoni Moda`.
